Are Adrian and Adam actually brother and sister?

Yes. The feet are also siblings.

Why do the toes move?

Each photo was run through a mesh-warp that flexes only the toe region with a travelling wave, then rendered to sprite sheets so the wiggle responds to your cursor. No feet were harmed. Several were flattered.

Can I book the feet?

The Tubero feet are not available for events, weddings, or product launches. They are available for lounging, and they are currently doing that.

Is it Tubero or Tibero?
