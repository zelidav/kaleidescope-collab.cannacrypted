Frequently asked

Questions.

Is this website real?

Regrettably, yes. You are looking at it.

Are Adrian and Adam actually brother and sister?

Yes. The feet are also siblings.

Why do the toes move when I touch them?

Each photo is stretched over a live mesh in your browser. A slow wave keeps the toes idling, and your finger or cursor pushes the mesh around in real time, so the toes squirm away from wherever you poke. No feet were harmed. Several were flattered.

Why do the toes make noise?

Each toe is tuned to a note of the pentatonic scale, big toe to pinky. Adam's are an octave lower because he is the brother. Play something.

Can I book the feet?

The Tubero feet are not available for events, weddings, or product launches. They are available for lounging, and they are currently doing that.

Is it Tubero or Tibero?

Tubero. With a U. As in “you should have known.”
